# Product overview

## Promise

“Read the package. Know where it belongs.” KierrätysAppi turns a barcode into explainable Finnish packaging guidance, without pretending incomplete product databases are authoritative.

## Implemented experience

- Finnish-first onboarding/home with English switch and no mandatory account.
- Contextual camera permission, EAN/UPC scanning, duplicate suppression and manual GTIN parity.
- Distinct loading, resolved, packaging-missing, not-found, provider-unavailable, invalid and offline states.
- Per-component destination, preparation, explanation, exceptions, source/check date and text confidence.
- Manual material/shape/deposit confirmation; ambiguous deposit/glass cases stay questions.
- Reviewed offline general packaging guide linked to Rinki.
- Opt-in, device-only recent history with clear/disable deletion behavior.
- Device-only correction drafts; no misleading active upload/moderation claim.
- Draft privacy/terms clearly marked for legal review.
- Material Ledger visual system, original deterministic icons, dark palette, scalable type and accessible controls.

## Deliberate exclusions

Cloud photo/OCR, general object recognition, maps, accounts/sync, community publication, rewards, manufacturer portal, organization API, GS1 production data, push notifications and production analytics are not activated. Each adds accuracy, consent, contract, moderation or infrastructure obligations that are not yet justified/approved.

## Success measures

Decode success, product-found rate, packaging coverage, actionable/verified result rate, latency, correction/incorrect-answer rate, camera-to-answer completion and accessibility completion are defined in the analytics dictionary. Raw barcode history is not an analytics property.
