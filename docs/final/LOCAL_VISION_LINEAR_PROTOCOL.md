## One fixed learned candidate

Eighteen input features are cosine similarities in the registered group order,
not model probabilities. Compute unweighted training-only population means and
standard deviations; replace SD≤1e-12 with1. Do not use validation statistics.

Fit six linear outputs jointly with an unpenalized intercept, using deterministic
Cholesky on the full19×19 normal system. The exact objective is the **SUM** of
the six class-mean squared residual losses plus `1 × ||W||²`; each row has weight
`1 / training_count_of_its_class`. There is no division by6, no softmax,
gradient/parameter search, cross-validation grid or validation-based calibration.
The unweighted centering does not make weighted intercept cross-terms zero;
solve them rather than assume it. Check finite factors/coefficients/residuals.
The constant model W=0 and every intercept1/6 has objective5, providing a solver
invariant, not image-accuracy evidence.
The runner independently reconstructs the actual training objective, slope norm
and weighted normal-equation residual before predicting. Require objective and
squared slope norm≤5+1e-8, and maximum absolute normal residual≤1e-8. A failure
is a numerical experiment failure, never a prediction-quality success or refit.

clipping or fake zero scores. Otherwise retain a known head winner only with a
unique top score and raw top-minus-second margin≥0.10. Negative or >1 outputs
are valid linear scores, **not factual confidence or probabilities**. The
