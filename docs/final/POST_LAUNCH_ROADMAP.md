# Post-launch roadmap

## Launch hardening

- Physical camera/accessibility/performance matrix and signed staged releases.
- Production PostgreSQL repositories/shared cache, content workflow, consented correction submission and authenticated moderation API.
- Privacy-safe aggregate reliability telemetry and crash reporting after processor/consent approval.
- Recurring Rinki/Palpa source review and accuracy incident workflow.

## Camera intelligence

- On-device OCR proof of concept for material codes and Finnish sorting text.
- Deterministic code/text parser with gold dataset, precision/recall and evidence spans.
- Local package/recycling-label capture with explicit purpose, metadata handling and deletion.
- Cloud multimodal fallback only after measurable need, explicit consent and overconfidence evaluation.

## Data coverage

- Licensed GS1 adapter per the integration plan.
- Verified manufacturer/internal records with source-aware resolution.
- Moderated community corrections and contributor transparency.
- Licensed Kierrätys.info collection points/local guidance when commercial terms permit.

## Expansion

- Municipality/country policy packs with jurisdiction/effective-date selection.
- Manufacturer portal and organization API after authentication, rate/billing/support and licence design.
- Favorites/sync only after account value, lawful basis, export/deletion and threat review.

Gamification, rewards, household impact metrics, push notifications and generalized object recognition remain excluded until evidence shows user value without misleading environmental claims or disproportionate data/energy cost.
