# User groups, jobs, and testable hypotheses

## Primary launch groups

| Group | Job to be done | Risk in the current experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer with an unfamiliar package | “Tell me where each piece goes without making me decode labels.” | Material, packaging status, deposit, and detachable parts are conflated |
| Visually impaired or cognitively overloaded person | “Give me a short, spoken, actionable answer and a fallback that does not depend on framing a barcode perfectly.” | Camera-first flows often hide manual parity and overload the result |
| Environmentally motivated household | “Help me be consistent without making exaggerated impact claims.” | Gamification can reward scans instead of correct outcomes |
| Student / young adult | “Resolve uncertainty at the bin quickly and without an account.” | Setup/account friction is larger than the immediate task |
| Multilingual resident | “Explain Finnish collection rules in language I understand.” | Waste vocabulary and local distinctions are hard to translate literally |

## Later organizational groups

Municipal waste advisers need versioned source/content workflows; retailers and manufacturers need correction/verification and data-entitlement paths; waste operators need jurisdictional rules and collection points; API customers need stable contracts and auditability.

## Highest-priority hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Test | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Component separation improves correct action | Compare single-bin result with component list on 10 multi-part packages | More components sorted correctly without longer median completion beyond budget |
| Source + plain-language status increases appropriate trust | Moderated test of verified/partial/ambiguous examples | Users can accurately say what is known vs uncertain |
| Contextual camera explanation improves permission recovery | Prototype test of pre-prompt and deny/retry paths | Users understand manual entry and can recover without reinstalling |
| Deposit ambiguity question prevents high-impact mistakes | Test cans/bottles with Finnish, foreign, and unclear marks | No silent deposit/material assumption; most users find the mark/check path |
| Finnish-first copy remains accessible at large text | Screen-reader and large-text task completion | Core flow completed without clipping, unlabeled controls, or camera dependency |
| OFF coverage is sufficient for a useful first cohort | Measure representative Finnish retail GTIN sample | Product-found, component coverage, actionable, verified-result rates reported separately |

Do not define a launch coverage target until a representative Finnish sample has been collected and licence-approved. A convenience sample cannot justify a market claim.
