## Product gap to pursue

KierrätysAppi's credible differentiation is not “AI recycling.” It is a calm Finnish answer that separates every known package component and visibly distinguishes:

- authoritative Finnish sorting policy from community product observations;
- verified facts from provider-derived or user-confirmed fields;
- a complete answer from a partial package record;
- deposit return from material collection;
- nationwide packaging rules from local collection-point availability;
- unknown/ambiguous next steps from fabricated convenience.

## What not to claim

No reviewed source establishes that competitors systematically expose field-level provenance, dates, calibrated confidence, component completeness, or AI evidence. That is a research hypothesis—not a public superiority claim—until tested in hands-on competitive review.
